About

I study how AI systems can follow explicit constraints, reason through multiple steps, and use verifiable feedback to produce reliable results.

I am a Research Assistant at AIDCEC, The Education University of Hong Kong, and a student in the Doctor of Artificial Intelligence (DAI) programme at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. My current research focuses on Text-to-CAD generation and geometric continuity, alongside interests in language-model reasoning, tool use, and agent memory.

Previously, I worked in data engineering, natural language processing, and large language models at Tencent, TAL Education Group, and an AI startup. I bring experience across model training, inference optimization, and application development to my research.

Current research

Ongoing work · titles and scope may evolve

01

Text2CAD-GC

A benchmark for intent-aware geometric continuity in Text-to-CAD

Developing a benchmark to evaluate whether generated CAD models satisfy continuity requirements at specified surface interfaces. The work covers STEP/B-Rep parsing, surface and interface representations, G0/G1/G2 evaluation, and reproducible evaluation protocols.

In progress · evaluator prototype and manuscript draft

02

Continuity-constrained CAD generation

Generating CAD that respects specified geometric requirements

Exploring structured surface and interface representations, explicit continuity instructions, and geometry-based feedback for generation and optimization. The objective is to satisfy the requested G0, G1, or G2 continuity while preserving intended shape and structure.

In progress · working research description
